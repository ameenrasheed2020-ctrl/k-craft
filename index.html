The gifting guide

Corporate gifting, explained.

Who do you gift?

Great programmes cover employees at every stage, clients at renewal time, channel partners during festivals, senior leaders, and remote teams who rarely meet in person. Each group needs a different tone and budget.

Gift ideas that work

Personalised stationery sets, tech accessories, gourmet hampers, wellness boxes, desk plants, crystal awards and experience vouchers. Pick items people will use daily so your brand stays visible.

Occasions to plan for

Onboarding, birthdays, work anniversaries, promotions, Diwali, year-end, Christmas, Women's Day, Doctor's Day and project milestones. A yearly calendar lets us lock pricing and delivery early.

FAQ

Questions companies ask before ordering.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Most collections start at 25 units. For smaller teams, ask about our ready-to-ship range.

Can you put our logo on the gifts and box?

Yes. We brand products and packaging using laser engraving, embossing, foil and print, and show a mockup before production.

How long does a bulk order take?

Typically 7 to 15 working days after mockup approval, depending on quantity and customisation. Book early for festive season.

Do you deliver to individual employees?

Yes. Share a spreadsheet of names and addresses and we ship each gift directly with tracking.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes. Volume pricing starts from 100 units and improves at 500 and 1,000 units.

Can you ship internationally?

Yes. We deliver to the USA, Canada, UAE, Singapore, Australia, Germany, the UK and more.

Do you provide GST invoices?

Yes. All orders include a GST invoice. Add your GSTIN in the enquiry list and we handle the rest.

Can we see a sample first?

Yes. We can send a physical sample of any kit before you confirm the full order.
